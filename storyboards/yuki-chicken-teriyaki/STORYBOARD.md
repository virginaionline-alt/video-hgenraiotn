## 1. Character bible: Yuki (locked)

**Identity (never changes)**
- Young adult anime woman with soft, delicate features.
- Long, thick, flowing **dark chocolate-brown wavy hair**, reaching past mid-back. Worn **half-up**: the top section is gathered at the back of the head with a **large ivory-white ribbon**, and the rest falls loose over her shoulders and back. Soft side-swept bangs part slightly off-centre.
- Large, expressive **dark brown eyes** with fine upper lashes and warm highlights.
- Soft **rosy cheeks**, a gentle closed-lip natural smile, and a small, slightly upturned nose.
- Small **pearl flower stud earrings** (five petals).

**Cooking outfit (never changes)**
- **Cream-white puff-sleeve blouse** in a soft gathered cotton. Sleeves are pushed up to the forearms while she cooks.
- **Sage-green floral apron** with a pale leaf-and-vine print, tied at the waist with a bow at the back.
- Visible natural fabric texture: cotton weave and soft creases.

**Movement and acting**
- Calm and unhurried, with graceful hands. She smiles softly to herself.
- Realistic cooking technique: claw grip on the knife, wrist-led whisking, and confident tong flips.
- Hands are always drawn with five fingers, natural proportions and short, clean, natural-pink nails.

**Consistency rule:** in every shot where she appears, check the hair length, hair colour, ribbon, earrings, eye colour and outfit against reference 1. Do not redesign her, change her hairstyle, tie it in a bun or add accessories.

## 2. Environment bible: the countryside kitchen

Use the layout of reference 2, adapted to a **gentle rainy afternoon** at about 3–4 pm.

**Inside (warm)**
- Warm wooden butcher-block countertop. **Sage-green shaker cabinets** with brass cup handles.
- **Hanging black enamel pendant lamp** with a warm bulb. This is the main warm key light.
- Open wooden shelves with glass spice jars, ceramic cups and hanging wooden spoons and cutting boards.
- Climbing ivy and white daisy-like flowers framing the window. A small vase of white flowers on the counter.
- A stainless gas stove on the right.
- A red-and-white checked tea towel, plus a sage leaf-print towel hanging on the oven handle.

**Outside (cool)**
- A large wood-framed window looking out over green meadows, a wooden fence, a red-roof farmhouse, a lake and misty blue mountains.
- **Gentle, continuous rainfall.** Soft grey sky, with mist on the mountains.
- Raindrops sliding down the glass, a little condensation at the lower corners, and leaves and flowers swaying lightly in the breeze.

**Companion**
- A **golden retriever** resting on a woven mat beneath the window. It stays calm and peaceful: small ear twitches, a slow tail thump and a sleepy head lift. **It never touches the food or interferes with the cooking.**

**Lighting rule (essential)**
- **Interior:** warm golden light (about 2700 K) from the pendant lamp, with soft amber bounce off the wood.
- **Exterior and window light:** cool blue-grey daylight (about 6500 K), giving a soft rim light on Yuki's left side.
- This **warm-inside / cool-outside contrast** must be visible in every wide and medium shot.

| Item | Locked appearance |

## 4. Sound design bible (ASMR)

- **Constant bed (0:00–1:00):** soft rain on the window glass and roof, an occasional gutter drip, and a very faint room tone. Keep it low under the foreground sounds, and never fully silent.
- **Foreground:** close-miked, crisp, intimate Foley that is lightly stereo-panned to match where the action is on screen.
- **Absolutely no** music, dialogue, humming, voice-over or sound-effect "stingers".
- **Mixing:** ending loudness of about -16 LUFS. Rain bed about 18 dB below peak foreground Foley. Sizzle peaks are softened so they never sound harsh.
